Limits

  • Synthetic data only. The simulator plants the traps the scorer handles.
  • School-day windows don't help the PQL score: calendar windows score about the same. The health false-alarm result is where school days prove themselves.
  • Stalled schools (budget freezes) look identical to buyers, so no usage score can avoid them. They are where the wrong PQL flags come from.
  • One shared calendar. Production needs a per-district calendar.
