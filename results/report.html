Limits

  • Synthetic data only. The simulator plants the traps the scorer handles.
  • School-day windows barely help PQL (same precision/recall). The health false-alarm story is the proof.
  • Stalled schools are unbeatable — they look identical to buyers. All 8 wrong PQL flags are stalled schools.
  • One shared calendar. Production needs a per-district calendar.
